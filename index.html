Leonida Clips

A personal tool that turns public GTA 6 Twitch highlight clips into vertical shorts, automatically shared to my own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

Private project UK-based No viewer data collected No cookies

What this is

Leonida Clips is a personal, non-commercial tool built and operated by a single individual in the United Kingdom. It identifies publicly available Twitch highlight clips related to GTA 6, edits them into short vertical-format videos, and publishes those edits to my own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts using each platform's official API.

What it isn't

This is not a public service, product, or business. There is no sign-up, no public account system, and nothing is sold. The tool is used solely by its creator to manage their own personal social media accounts.

Credit and removal

Every clip published through this tool credits the original streamer it was sourced from. If you are a streamer and want a clip removed, contact me and it will be taken down promptly.

Data handling

Account tokens used to post to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram are stored only on my own computer. No viewer data is collected, and this site uses no cookies or tracking of any kind.
