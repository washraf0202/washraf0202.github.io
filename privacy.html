Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Leonida Clips ("the tool") is a private, non-commercial project operated by a single individual in the United Kingdom. This policy explains how the tool handles data.

Who operates this tool

Leonida Clips is built, run and used solely by its creator. It is not a public service and has no user accounts, sign-up process, or customer base. If you have any question about this policy, contact mohammadashraf020297@gmail.com.

What the tool does

The tool finds publicly posted Twitch highlight clips related to GTA 6, edits them into vertical-format short videos, and publishes those edits to the creator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, using each platform's official API.

Data we collect

This tool collects no data about viewers of the published videos or visitors to this website. No analytics, no tracking pixels, and no cookies are used on this site or within the tool.

Account tokens

To post to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram on the creator's behalf, the tool uses API access tokens issued by those platforms. These tokens are stored only locally, on the creator's own computer. They are never uploaded to a server, shared with any third party, or sold.

Source clip data

The tool only processes clips that are already publicly available on Twitch. It does not collect private data, does not scrape viewer information, and does not access any Twitch account other than what is needed to read public clip data via Twitch's official API.

No sale of data

Nothing collected or processed by this tool is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers. There is no commercial use of any data whatsoever.

Credit to streamers

Every clip published through this tool credits the original streamer it came from.

Removal requests

If you are a streamer and want a clip featuring your content removed from any platform this tool posts to, email mohammadashraf020297@gmail.com with a link to the clip and it will be removed promptly.

Children's privacy

This tool is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in how the tool operates. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.

Governing law
